What is Sapio?

Sapio is an anagram of Open Source API. Sapio tells you whether an Android app works on a device without Google Play Services, that is, a deGoogled bare AOSP device, coupled or not with microG.

Sapio's evaluations are made by the community, for the community. Sapio can serve as a lobbying tool: by sharing compatibility on social media, you raise awareness among app developers about respecting users' personal data.
